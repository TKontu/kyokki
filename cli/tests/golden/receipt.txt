usage: kyokki receipt [-h] COMMAND ...

Receipts: scan to stock. Upload a photo or PDF, wait for the model
to read it, then confirm the lines it matched to a product.

options:
  -h, --help  show this help message and exit

commands:
  COMMAND
    upload    upload a receipt image or PDF for processing
    status    a receipt's processing status, and its lines once read
    confirm   confirm the receipt's matched lines into stock

examples:
  kyokki receipt upload receipt.jpg --wait
  kyokki receipt status 0b6f7a3e-8d4c-4a53-9d1e-2f6c1b7e9a01
  kyokki receipt confirm 0b6f7a3e-8d4c-4a53-9d1e-2f6c1b7e9a01 --all-matched

exit codes:
  0 ok
  1 error: cannot reach the server, a 5xx, an answer that is not JSON, or a
    change sent that got no answer (it may have been applied)
  2 usage: bad arguments, or the API rejected the request (400 invalid, 422)
  3 not found: no receipt has the ID
  4 ambiguous name: does not occur for this command
  5 insufficient stock: does not occur for this command
  6 conflict: a duplicate upload, an unready receipt, unmatched food lines, or a
    retried confirm (409/400; confirm has no Idempotency-Key)
  7 auth: missing or unknown token (401), or a read token on a write (403)
